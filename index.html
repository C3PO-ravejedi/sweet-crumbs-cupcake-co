Our Story

Founded in 2024 by a group of pastry enthusiasts, NAME began as a tiny kitchen in Brooklyn’s industrial district. The mission was simple: bring the joy of perfectly baked cupcakes to every neighborhood, using only the finest, locally‑sourced ingredients. Over the past two years we have grown from a weekend pop‑up to a beloved community staple, serving hundreds of customers daily.

Our bakers experiment with seasonal fruits, exotic spices, and sustainable packaging to keep the menu fresh and the planet happy. We partner with local farms for organic eggs, free‑range butter, and heirloom wheat flour. Every cupcake is hand‑decorated, ensuring each bite feels like a small celebration.

Beyond the bake shop, we host weekly cupcake‑making workshops, support local schools with fundraising events, and donate unsold treats to nearby shelters. Our commitment to community, quality, and creativity drives everything we do, and we can’t wait to share that passion with you.

Looking ahead, we plan to launch a subscription box, expand to neighboring boroughs, and introduce gluten‑free and vegan options without compromising flavor. Stay tuned for exciting new flavors and collaborations that will keep your taste buds dancing.
